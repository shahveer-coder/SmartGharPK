How we started

Our Story

Smart Ghar started with a simple observation: Pakistani families were paying too much for everyday home products — and often receiving items that broke within weeks. We wanted to change that with a brand people could actually trust.

So we began hand-picking kitchen and home gadgets, testing each one in real kitchens, comparing quality, and negotiating directly with manufacturers to keep prices honest. Only products we would use in our own homes made the cut.

Today Smart Ghar delivers across Pakistan with cash on delivery, WhatsApp support before and after your purchase, and a straightforward 7-day return policy. No gimmicks — just good products, fairly priced and delivered on time.
